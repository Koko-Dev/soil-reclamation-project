The Soil Is Dying. So Are Our Carrots.

Did You Know?

It may take three carrots today to equal the nutrients in one carrot from the 1940s. This isn't folklore — it's a real consequence of lifeless soil.

Most lawns today are starving the land. The roots run shallow, the topsoil blows away, and the only thing thriving is the weed killer industry.

We have a better way. A way that restores nutrients, builds topsoil, and brings biodiversity back to our neighborhoods.

It starts in your backyard. With a rake, a few seeds, and the decision to stop growing grass and start growing life.

Replace Grass with Plants That Heal

  • White Clover — fixes nitrogen, soft underfoot, survives drought.
  • Creeping Thyme — fragrant, walkable, pollinator-friendly.
  • Hairy Vetch — breaks up compacted soil and restores fertility.
  • Buckwheat — grows fast, suppresses weeds, adds biomass.
  • Yarrow — deep taproots, drought-hardy, supports insect allies.
